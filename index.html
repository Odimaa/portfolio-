AI + Brand

GEO Audit Framework

GEO stands for Generative Engine Optimization. It is the practice of making sure your brand shows up accurately and prominently when people ask AI tools questions. Not Google. ChatGPT. Gemini. Claude. Perplexity. That is where a lot of people are going first now, and most brands have no idea how they appear there.

I designed and ran BellaNaija's first GEO audit across all four major platforms, scoring how the brand was cited, identifying where it was missing, and building a monthly process to track it going forward.
