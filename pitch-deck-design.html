WHAT'S INCLUDED

Everything the document needs, in one place

Most people come with raw material, a career history, a campaign they ran, a pitch they need to make. I take that and handle everything it takes to turn it into something that works.

Finding the thread that connects your experience, your work, and what you want the reader to do next. This is where most documents fall apart, and where I start.

Bio, case study framing, impact language, service descriptions. Written in your voice, not mine.

Clean, typographic, built to hold up in a boardroom or an inbox. No generic templates.

Your numbers presented so the impact is legible. Impressions, partnership counts, campaign reach, revenue, whatever you have.

Colour, type, and layout choices that feel intentional and consistent across every page. Not decoration, structure.
